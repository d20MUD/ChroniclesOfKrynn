# Chronicles of Krynn changelog: June 7–October 14, 2026

Prepared October 16, 2026. Covers committed changes from June 7 through the latest
repository commit, `f1bebe6c` (October 14). The first commit in this interval is
June 10. This is a source-history report; live deployment and world configuration
have not been verified. Dates in the commit appendix use America/Halifax time.

## Most impactful changes

### Two new prestige classes

- **Dragon Disciple:** a ten-level prestige class with draconic bloodline progression,
  natural attacks, breath, wings, blindsense, and Dragon Form. Follow-up fixes route
  perk awards to the character's actual base classes instead of always Sorcerer.
  [Merged PR #8](https://github.com/d20MUD/ChroniclesOfKrynn/pull/8),
  [perk correction #15](https://github.com/d20MUD/ChroniclesOfKrynn/pull/15).
- **Loremaster:** a ten-level prestige class combining selectable secrets, Lore,
  Greater Lore, True Lore, bonus languages, and a choice of eligible existing
  spellcasting class to advance at each level. The implementation was authored in
  July and merged in August.
  [Merged PR #17](https://github.com/d20MUD/ChroniclesOfKrynn/pull/17).

### Housing, storage, and a player economy

- Buy and expand a home in a staff-configured neighbourhood; customize rooms,
  manage guest access, and purchase persistent storage chests.
- A fixed-price auction house supports player listings, purchases, repricing,
  cancellation, recovery, and collection. Use `ah <command>` at a configured
  auction location; neighbourhood and auction availability require world setup.
- Direct `trade` supports items, gold, materials, and motes with review and
  acceptance by both participants. `rejecttrades` disables incoming requests.
- Sources: [housing and auction implementation](https://github.com/d20MUD/ChroniclesOfKrynn/commit/00ec6eca),
  [trade implementation](https://github.com/d20MUD/ChroniclesOfKrynn/commit/4a6eb5a8),
  [housing guide](housing-auction-port.md), [trade guide](guides/TRADE_COMMAND_HELP.md).

### Permanent account rewards

- Spend account XP on permanent bonuses to XP, treasure drops, gold, and quest points.
  Each increment costs 2,000 account XP and adds 1%, up to 10% per category.
- `accexp bonus` displays bonuses; `accexp bonus <type>` buys an upgrade. Bonuses
  persist and update across logged-in characters. Treasure bonuses add percentage
  points to drop chance.
- [Implementation](https://github.com/d20MUD/ChroniclesOfKrynn/commit/f315e524).

### Companions that grow and can return after defeat

- Newly purchased pets earn independent combat XP without taking player XP and
  advance up to the owner's level or level 30. Older unmarked purchases are not
  automatically enrolled in purchased-pet leveling.
- Eligible permanent companions earn training ranks and skill points for health,
  attack, damage, armor, or saves. Training caps at rank 10 and does not change
  class companions' creature levels.
- Permanent owned companions are stored on death with progression, equipment,
  inventory, and gold. `pets fallen` lists them; `pets revive <ID>` restores one
  for 500 carried gold per pet level, subject to follower space and combat checks.
- Crafted golems retain their identity after login, use a separate follower
  allowance, have improved healing, and support appearance customization.
- [Progression implementation](https://github.com/d20MUD/ChroniclesOfKrynn/commit/080b0fe8),
  [companion guide](purchased_pet_progression.md).

### Tactical group formations

- `formation` places group members in front, middle, or back rows. Leaders can
  position other members, including companions. Moving rows in combat costs a move action.
- Melee attacks require the front; reach weapons work from the middle; ranged
  attacks work from the back. Front-row ranged attacks have feat-dependent penalties.
- Ordinary NPC melee reaches the nearest occupied row, large enemies reach two
  rows, and huge enemies reach all three. Ranged attacks and area effects still
  use their normal rules. Positions reset on leaving a group or logging in.
- [Implementation](https://github.com/d20MUD/ChroniclesOfKrynn/commit/7717f436),
  [formation help](../lib/text/help/formation.hlp).

### Krynn's moons now shape eligible arcane casting

- Lunar phases and genuine conjunctions affect spell DC, bonus spells, and effective
  caster level for eligible characters. Knights of the Thorn remain exempt.
- `score` shows combined modifiers; `look moons` reports visible conjunctions;
  `help moons` explains the system. The Night of the Eye requires a genuine triple
  conjunction at High Sanction.
- Corrected inconsistent lunar phase tracking and the save modifier's direction;
  recalculation preserves spent bonus slots and avoids stacking bonuses repeatedly.
- [Implementation](https://github.com/d20MUD/ChroniclesOfKrynn/commit/1a7741b7),
  [moon mechanics](moon_conjunctions.md).

## Crafting, harvesting, and loot

- Disenchant eligible magical items into catalyst fragments; catalysts can improve
  crafted items beyond weapons and armor. Introduced in late June.
- Batch refining reduces repetitive production work.
- Zone climate and terrain support a wider range of harvested material variants.
  A material-exchange special procedure supports one-for-one exchanges within the
  same material type and grade where configured.
- Rare materials and motes can drop alongside ordinary random treasure and save
  directly to crafting balances. Higher grades unlock with enemy level; grade 5
  materials and dragonmetal require level 30+ enemies and remain especially rare.
- Insightful grants mining, hunting, forestry, gathering, and butchering 20% bonus
  skill XP per rank, reaching 100% at five ranks.
- Happy-hour bonuses now consistently apply to crafting/gathering skill XP,
  refining, harvested materials, butchering, and mote quantities. Fixed duplicate
  refining XP and rates persisting after happy hour ends. Harvest messages show
  total yields. Single crafted equipment items remain single items.
- Abandoning supply orders refunds materials and clears project state; completion
  clears stale recipes. Old offices no longer issue obsolete orders.
- Hoopak crafting accepts an equipped weaponsmith's hammer. Universal and
  enhancement AC enchantments now require and display water motes.
- Reforging no longer grants general XP or advances obsolete crafting skills.
- Sources: commits `4cc7b5c1`, `5f4fd9ba`, `0abd79b3`, `6ba2d502`, `6a0c767b`,
  `2ad3cebf`, `66789d53`, `51058122`, `2f584331`, `9407a702`, `93b54219`, `f896e10a`;
  [happy-hour details](happy_hour_crafting.md).

## Classes, spells, feats, and combat

- Clerics gain spontaneous casting; domain perk effects, smite, and group shield
  spells received fixes. `domain spells` lists granted spells with circle and
  minimum level; preparation reports remaining bonus domain slots.
- Added Feral Mutagen natural attacks and Multiattack. Alchemist bombs use ranged
  attack feats correctly and show attack rolls and damage dice. Faster Bomb
  Preparation reduces preparation cooldown to three seconds. Preparation shows
  current/maximum bombs. Ordinary extracts work in silence and soundproof rooms.
- Alchemists gain Apply Poison alongside Swift Poisoning at level 6; corrected
  `applypoison` command routing and feat checks.
- Added Practiced Spellcaster: up to four caster levels for a selected casting
  class, capped by total character level; it grants no new spell circles or slots.
- Improved epic spell damage, effects, and access. July changes tie epic slots to
  Spellcraft and regenerate one slot per three minutes. Corrected Epic Mage Armor.
- Added Litany of Damnation; fixed Litany of Righteousness and Touch of Undeath.
- Expanded warrior bonus critical feats and increased perk costs by one point.
  Corrected Weapon Training critical triggers and unintended monk sneak attacks.
- Weapon Finesse applies to unarmed strikes; estocs work as finesse weapons.
  Added estoc, fauchard, elven curve blade, and falcata weapon types. Staves can
  be wielded with one or two hands.
- Monk armor penalties apply to the appropriate abilities. Defensive Strike and
  Bastion use working cooldowns; daily-use helpers reject invalid feat indices.
- Fixed wizard metamagic, Arcane Recovery, Recall Spell, Master of Elements,
  and Arcane Supremacy perks. Prestige-class perk spending no longer incorrectly
  requires levels in the associated base class.
- Blindsense and blindsight follow the implemented Pathfinder 1e rules.
- Fixed Psionic Focus reducing damage, Psionic Blast stun allowing combat turns,
  and Deadly Fear failing to become area damage at augmentation 8 or above.
- Shadowcasting no longer requires concentration checks. Dazzling Display
  affects eligible enemies instead of its user. Master Assassin checks prerequisites.
- `affects` shows remaining Stoneskin, Ironskin, and Epic Warding protection;
  recasting refreshes protection correctly. Lich Touch appears in cooldowns.
- Dragon riders gain Dragon Flight using a dragoon point; dragon mounts can be
  ordered to fly. Eligible aquatic creatures/forms automatically succeed at
  ordinary swimming; boat-only and ocean travel restrictions still apply.
- Longer summoned-companion duration; followers of followers assist the player
  master. Cross-room ranged kills complete without entering the victim's room.
- Typed equipment feats use and display their assigned weapon family, skill,
  or spell school. Artificers can scribe eligible Wizard spells without memorizing
  them, using Artificer level. Fixed stored scrolls and wand spell-name parsing.
- Corrected Greater Mirror Image messaging, Acid Fog cascading/per-round damage,
  placeholder spell names, bozak death throes, and fog-room behavior.

## Quests, hunts, commands, and presentation

- Group members on the same quest share objective completion; companion kills
  credit their owner. Sanction/dialogue quest fixes prevent accidental progress
  loss and correctly follow successful fallback/next-quest chains.
- Hunts reject unreachable candidate rooms, avoid duplicate targets moved from
  their spawn room, and use the dragon's configured breath element.
- Added up to ten buff lists and expanded special spell arguments. Adjusted NPC
  automatic detection buffs; wizard pre-buffing supports eligible True Seeing.
- Information/chat commands such as `news`, `motd`, and `who` remain usable during
  device creation/repair. `device move <from> <to>` persists ordering without losing
  charges, cooldowns, or condition.
- Editor saving now uses `/s`; `@` and `/@` no longer save, preserving lines that
  begin with those characters or color codes.
- Restored `statscap`; added perk lookup through `help`. Page length and screen
  width support values up to 200.
- Fixed saved pet targeting keywords, hidden-NPC detection, bard instrument checks,
  Dance of Protection, bard song duration, single-equip item flags, shopkeeper
  price replies, quest-master markers, color bleeding, and linkdead 256-color persistence.
- Linkdead characters are extracted after five minutes.

## Staff, build, and campaign-specific work

- Added `questcomplete` for quest recovery and `vnum r` for room searches; mob
  statistics editing supports adjustments by level range.
- Added housing/auction schema, optional help migrations, environment editing,
  and targeted regression tooling. Build documentation explains regenerating
  Autotools files when versioned tools are missing; compiler warnings were addressed.
- **Forgotten Realms build only:** map-derived Faerûn wilderness generation,
  terrain/road interpretation, mapping, and related movement/hunt integration.
  This is shared-code campaign work, not evidence of a new Krynn world area.
