# Chronicles of Krynn changelog — June 26–November 7, 2026

Overview of implemented changes merged during this period. Related fixes are
combined; availability of features that require world setup may vary.

## Characters, races, and advancement

- Added the Loremaster prestige class, including secrets, lore abilities, languages, and spellcasting advancement.
- Added Irda, Sivak Draconian, and Aurak Draconian as advanced race choices.
- Added persistent Irda shapechanging and customizable assumed appearances.
- Added the Irda racial abilities Augment Physicality and Mirage Flare.
- Added Sivak stolen appearances, saved collections of forms, and racial flight and combat abilities.
- Added Aurak racial spellcasting, energy rays, noxious breath, and death throes.
- Refined Aurak innate spell costs, recovery, action use, and separation from class spellcasting.
- Fixed Bozak death throes and several draconian racial-ability issues.
- Gave Sorcerers their own perk tables and migrated eligible existing perk investments.
- Fixed Dragon Disciple perk awards and prestige-class access to associated base-class perks.
- Corrected advancement, perk-point, and level-gain messages.
- Increased perk purchase costs.
- Added Practiced Spellcaster and expanded critical-combat feat choices for martial classes.
- Added permanent account-wide bonuses for experience, treasure, gold, and quest points.

## Introductions, disguises, and identity

- Completed the introduction system with remembered names and introduction management.
- Added customizable mundane disguises with observer-specific detection.
- Expanded Disguise Self and Alter Self support and appearance controls.
- Added Shifter Guise, granting Shifters access to Disguise Self and Alter Self.
- Expanded True Sight to distinguish natural forms, transformed forms, and disguises.
- Added anonymous listings for players who want to conceal their identity from strangers.
- Updated targeting, descriptions, pronouns, communication, and group messages to respect visible identities.
- Fixed hidden-NPC detection and corrected blindsense and blindsight behavior.

## Housing, trading, and player shops

- Added direct player trading of items, gold, crafting materials, and motes.
- Added trade review and acceptance, cancellation handling, and a preference to reject trade requests.
- Added player-purchased neighbourhood housing with expandable room layouts.
- Added house guest access and customizable room titles, descriptions, and extra descriptions.
- Added persistent housing chests, including storage of nested items and sheathed weapons.
- Added house renaming through `house name` and `house rename`.
- Added a fixed-price auction house with listings, purchases, repricing, cancellation, recovery, and proceeds collection.
- Added rentable player market stalls with offline selling, shop customization, stock management, and rent handling.
- Added staff controls for neighbourhoods, auction locations, and player markets.
- Improved transaction recovery and protection against lost items, duplicate transfers, and repeated payouts.

## Legendary equipment

- Added the Dragonmetal Awakening legendary-item system and the `infuse` command.
- Added equipment paths, rank advancement, selectable passive bonuses, techniques, signatures, and modifiers.
- Added independent primary and supporting channels for eligible two-handed equipment.
- Added infusion experience, legendary materials, boss rewards, and material exchanges.
- Added loadout inspection, passive priorities, power attunement, and activation controls.
- Added owner binding, storage compatibility, persistent cooldowns, and safeguards for legendary-item transactions.
- Added staff feature controls, encounter eligibility, diagnostics, and release-support tools.

## Combat, weapons, and ammunition

- Reworked ranged combat around same-room targeting and weapon range.
- Added combat distance, room-size-based starting distances, and combat advancement.
- Added automatic approach behavior for NPCs and companions, with ranged-attack support for eligible mobs.
- Added group formations with front, middle, and back ranks.
- Integrated melee reach, ranged attacks, and formation positioning.
- Added ammunition supplies with quantities, enhancement bonuses, elemental damage, and unlimited-ammunition support.
- Added wielded thrown-weapon combat and returning-ammunition support.
- Reworked Point-Blank Shot, Precise Shot, Improved Precise Shot, Rapid Shot, Manyshot, Far Shot, Quick Draw, Deadly Aim, and reload interactions.
- Added estocs, fauchards, elven curve blades, and falcatas; corrected estoc finesse handling.
- Added one-handed and two-handed staff wielding.
- Fixed Weapon Finesse with unarmed attacks and corrected several light-weapon checks.
- Fixed unintended Weapon Training criticals and monk sneak attacks.
- Corrected damage reduction from equipment, spells, and effects.
- Fixed Dazzling Display targeting, Stunning Blow saves, and Master Assassin prerequisites.
- Stopped poisoned status from breaking stealth.
- Corrected cross-room ranged-kill completion before the later ranged-system overhaul.

## Magic, psionics, and class abilities

- Added spontaneous spell conversion for Clerics.
- Added domain-spell listings and clearer reporting of bonus domain slots.
- Implemented lunar phases and conjunction bonuses for eligible arcane casters.
- Reworked epic spell strength, preparation, cast recovery, and epic-summon abilities.
- Added Litany of Damnation, Protection from Poison, and Panacea.
- Fixed Litany of Righteousness, Touch of Undeath, Epic Mage Armor, Acid Fog, and Greater Mirror Image.
- Corrected fog-room behavior and associated effects.
- Fixed Wizard metamagic, Arcane Recovery, Spell Recall, Master of Elements, Arcane Supremacy, and related perk behavior.
- Fixed Sorcerer spell selection, spell-slot allocation, and casting progression.
- Fixed Psionic Focus damage, psionic saving-throw difficulties, Psionic Blast stunning, and Deadly Fear augmentation.
- Fixed Arcane Augmentation and related spell and power interactions.
- Enabled Holy Weapon benefits for unarmed attacks.
- Removed the Clone spell's component requirement.
- Removed concentration checks from shadowcasting.
- Fixed refreshing of Stoneskin, Ironskin, and Epic Warding protection.
- Adjusted NPC automatic detection buffs and True Seeing use.
- Improved bard instrument recognition, Dance of Protection, and performance duration.
- Removed unused spell placeholders from player-facing spell information.

## Alchemy and artificer devices

- Improved bomb preparation feedback and added Faster Bomb Preparation.
- Corrected ranged-feat and accuracy interactions for bombs.
- Added clearer bomb attack and damage feedback.
- Allowed ordinary alchemical extracts to work silently, including in silenced areas.
- Corrected Apply Poison access and command routing for Alchemists.
- Allowed Artificers to scribe eligible Wizard spells without memorizing them.
- Added saved device reordering without losing device state.
- Allowed supported information and chat commands during device creation and repair.

## Pets, companions, and travel

- Added experience-based progression for purchased pets.
- Added training ranks and selectable improvements for eligible permanent companions.
- Added fallen-companion storage, revival, and discard handling.
- Preserved companion progression, equipment, inventory, and gold through supported saves and revival.
- Added companion advancement information to pet and group displays.
- Fixed pet targeting keywords, loading, and progression persistence.
- Allowed followers of companions to assist their ultimate player owner.
- Increased summoned-companion duration.
- Improved crafted-golem healing, identity restoration, repair eligibility, and follower allowances.
- Added crafted-golem appearance customization.
- Added Dragon Rider flight magic and support for ordering dragon mounts to fly.
- Added automatic ordinary swimming for eligible aquatic companions and transformed creatures.

## Crafting, harvesting, and loot

- Added disenchanting of eligible magical items into catalyst fragments.
- Added crafting catalysts and expanded their use beyond weapons and armour.
- Added batch refining.
- Added zone climate and terrain support for varied harvesting materials.
- Added material exchanges between compatible material types and grades.
- Added rare crafting-material and mote drops alongside ordinary treasure.
- Increased Insightful harvesting talent benefits.
- Applied happy-hour bonuses consistently to crafting, harvesting, refining, butchering, and mote rewards.
- Corrected happy-hour bonus stacking, expiry, and yield reporting.
- Fixed Elbow Grease and Jack of All Trades bonuses in affected crafting activities.
- Corrected supply-order item selection, obsolete orders, refunds, completion, and project cleanup.
- Fixed hoopak crafting-tool checks and armour-enchantment mote requirements.
- Removed unintended experience and obsolete skill gains from reforging.
- Corrected randomly assigned equipment-feat specializations and their descriptions.
- Removed the obsolete `practice` command and refreshed crafting tutorials and guides.

## Quests, hunts, and clans

- Added quest sharing between players.
- Added shared objective completion for eligible group members on the same quest.
- Credited companion quest kills to their owners.
- Fixed dialogue and Sanction quest progress loss and fallback or follow-up quest handling.
- Improved quest-item turn-ins and preserved quest-master indicators on shopkeepers.
- Added hunt-location reachability checks and prevented duplicate displaced hunt targets.
- Corrected hunt dragons' breath damage types.
- Added pending clan-application notifications for staff and clan members.
- Added approval and rejection of offline clan applicants.
- Expanded clan notifications for membership, leadership, and territory changes.

- Added `spellhelp` with spell and psionic-power information based on implemented mechanics.
- Added perk lookup through the help command and a database export of feat information.
- Added multiple saved buff lists and expanded support for spells with special buff arguments.
- Added replies to received in-game mail.
- Restored stat-cap reporting and expanded `defenses` with spell resistance, concealment, and damage reduction.
- Updated Lore checks to use the best eligible knowledge skill.
- Added remaining-protection information for absorption effects and Lich Touch cooldown reporting.
- Improved damage feedback for slam and draconian bite attacks and visibility of flying characters.
- Increased supported screen width and page length.
- Changed editor saving to `/s`, preserving lines that begin with `@` or colour codes.
- Fixed colour bleeding and persistence of 256-colour preferences after disconnection.
- Fixed stored-scroll handling, wand spell-name parsing, and shopkeeper price replies.

- Expanded VNUM searches to rooms and quests.
- Improved BuildWalk to reconnect overlapping rooms instead of creating duplicates.

- Added automatic saving and extraction of characters left linkdead for an extended period.

- Addressed compiler warnings and build-system compatibility issues.
