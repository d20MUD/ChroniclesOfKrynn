# Chronicles of Krynn changelog — October 26–November 7, 2026

Overview of implemented changes from October 26 onward, inclusive. Related fixes
are combined, and features introduced before this period are not listed as new.
Some additions require world configuration before becoming available.

## Legendary equipment

- Added the Dragonmetal Awakening legendary-item system and the `infuse` command.
- Added equipment paths, rank advancement, selectable passive bonuses, techniques, signatures, and modifiers.
- Added independent primary and supporting channels for eligible two-handed equipment.
- Added infusion experience, legendary materials, boss rewards, and material exchanges.
- Added item inspection, upgrade guidance, loadout displays, passive priorities, attunement, and power activation.
- Added quoted purchases, confirmation, reshaping, and safeguards against duplicate charges or item changes during transactions.
- Added owner binding and legendary-item restrictions across trading, shops, storage, and item handling.
- Added persistent cooldowns, resource limits, item identities, and transaction recovery.
- Added staff feature controls, legendary boss eligibility, diagnostics, and release-support tools.
- Refined infusion help, material selection, rank guidance, and earning limits.

## Combat, ranged weapons, and ammunition

- Reworked ranged combat around same-room targeting and weapon range.
- Added room-size-based combat distance and the combat `advance` command.
- Added automatic approach behavior for NPCs and companions and ranged-attack support for eligible mobs.
- Updated formation interactions with melee reach, ranged attacks, and combat movement.
- Added ammunition supplies with quantities, enhancements, elemental damage, and unlimited-ammunition support.
- Added wielded thrown-weapon combat and returning-ammunition support.
- Reworked Point-Blank Shot, Precise Shot, Improved Precise Shot, Rapid Shot, Manyshot, Far Shot, Quick Draw, Deadly Aim, and reload interactions.
- Corrected separation of weapon attacks, ammunition use, and ranged spell or touch attacks.
- Fixed Stunning Blow saving throws.
- Stopped poisoned status from breaking stealth.
- Added damage feedback for slam and draconian bite attacks.

## Spellcasting, psionics, and advancement

- Added `spellhelp` with spell and psionic-power information based on implemented mechanics.
- Refined spell-help output and removed unnecessary information.
- Gave Sorcerers independent perk tables and migrated eligible existing perk investments.
- Fixed Sorcerer spell selection, spell-slot allocation, and casting progression.
- Corrected shared arcane-perk handling and Arcane Augmentation behavior.
- Fixed psionic saving-throw difficulties and Deadly Fear augmentation behavior.
- Enabled Holy Weapon benefits for unarmed attacks.
- Removed the Clone spell's component requirement.
- Corrected perk-point and level-gain messages.
- Updated Lore checks to use the best eligible knowledge skill.

## Racial abilities

- Added the Irda racial abilities Augment Physicality and Mirage Flare.
- Corrected advanced-race registration and racial-ability handling.
- Added resource costs, recovery, and persistent use tracking for applicable Aurak innate spells.
- Fixed Aurak spellcasting and removed inappropriate racial spells from class spell-selection lists.

## Companions and persistence

- Fixed companion saving, loading, progression, and ownership handling.
- Preserved main-eidolon training through dismissal, replacement bodies, and resummoning.
- Prevented temporary or stale eidolon copies from overwriting saved training progress.
- Added clearer companion experience-to-next-advancement information to pet and group displays.
- Improved equipment saving during logout, idle extraction, link loss, and copyover.
- Added more robust object checkpoints and handling of failed saves to reduce accidental item loss.
- Improved preservation of item properties through storage and serialization.

## Crafting and supply orders

- Fixed Elbow Grease bonuses in affected crafting and harvesting activities.
- Fixed Jack of All Trades bonuses in affected skill and crafting checks.
- Corrected supply-order item selection and project validation.
- Removed the obsolete `practice` command.
- Updated the crafting tutorial, player guides, and website-ready crafting documentation.

## Clans and communication

- Added pending clan-application notifications for staff and clan members.
- Added approval and rejection of offline clan applicants.
- Added applicant decision notifications and expanded clan membership, leadership, and territory notices.
- Added replies to received in-game mail, including private replies to announcements.

## Housing and player markets

- Added house renaming through `house name` and `house rename`.
- Added staff removal of a room's market stalls while preserving former tenants' stock, earnings, and history.
- Improved market-directory handling of removed stalls.
- Refreshed housing administration help and player-market documentation.

## Staff and builder tools

- Improved BuildWalk to reconnect overlapping rooms instead of creating duplicates.
- Added editable room sizes and preserved them through room copying and saving.
- Added builder controls for ammunition quantities, enhancements, elemental damage, and returning or unlimited supplies.
- Added the ranged-attack mob flag for same-room ranged encounters.
- Added `zpromote` for copying saved dev zones to live, including read-only previews.
- Added timestamped backups of live zone files and world indexes before promotion.
- Added backup listing and restoration, with a new backup of the current live zone before every restore.
- Added checksum verification, shared-index preservation, and rollback handling for zone transfers and restores.
- Supported running zone transfers and restores from either configured port while keeping the transfer direction fixed as dev to live.

## Help, data, and maintenance

- Added automatic export of feat definitions and prerequisites to the database.
- Expanded help and guides for legendary equipment, racial abilities, companion progression, ranged combat, ammunition, room sizes, mail, and zone promotion.
- Added database migrations, release diagnostics, and regression coverage for the new and corrected systems.
