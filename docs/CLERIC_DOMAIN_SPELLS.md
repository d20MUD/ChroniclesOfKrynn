## Browsing domains

- `domains` lists the available domains in three columns.
- `domains info <name>` shows a domain's description, favored weapon, granted powers, and spells by circle. Names are case-insensitive and accept unambiguous abbreviations.
- `domains spells` lists spells granted by your own domains and indicates whether you can prepare them yet.

The command is registered as `domains`; the normal command abbreviation rules still allow `domain`.
