SORCERER BLOODLINES AND HERITAGE OPTIONS
Chronicles of Krynn - current codebase overview

- General notes
  - There are 10 bloodlines, numbered as they appear in Study.
  - Arcane, Draconic and Elemental have heritage suboptions. The others do not.
  - All levels below refer to sorcerer levels.
  - Bonus feat lists are available choices, not automatic grants of every feat.
  - For Shadow, Stormborn, Destined, Abyssal, Elemental and Celestial, the nine bonus
    spells listed become known at levels 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17 and 19,
    respectively. They use normal spell slots, without using Study choices.
  - The original four bloodlines retain their existing bonus-spell rules.
  - Study shows benefits before asking you to accept or reject a bloodline.
  - All use limits below are per five real minutes unless explicitly stated otherwise.
    Each pool resets five minutes after its first use; retries do not restart the timer.
    Conviction instead recovers every 30 seconds.

- 0) ARCANE
  - Focus: Metamagic, spell versatility and school specialization.
  - Class skill: Appraise.
  - Heritage suboptions: Choose one school. School Power at level 15 grants +2 spell
    save DC for that school, stacking with Spell Focus.
    - 1) Abjuration: Protective magic, wards and dispelling.
    - 2) Conjuration: Summoning and conjured effects.
    - 3) Divination: Detection, information and magical sight.
    - 4) Enchantment: Charms, compulsions and mental influence.
    - 5) Evocation: Direct magical energy and destructive effects.
    - 6) Illusion: Deception, invisibility and illusory effects.
    - 7) Necromancy: Death magic, life draining and undead effects.
    - 8) Transmutation: Changing creatures, objects and physical capabilities.
  - Level 1 - Arcane Arcana: Metamagic-enhanced spells gain +1 save DC.
  - Level 1 - Improved Familiar: Grants one familiar improvement rank: +1 AC, +10 HP and
    +1 Strength, Dexterity and Constitution.
  - Level 3 - Metamagic Adept: Can negate metamagic's extra casting time when activated
    during casting. Example: cast maximize metamagicadept 'fireball'.
  - Level 9 - New Arcana: Additional spell-slot benefits for circles 1-3; improves at
    level 13 to circles 1-6 and level 17 to circles 1-9.
  - Level 15 - School Power: +2 save DC for your chosen school's spells.
  - Level 20 - Arcane Apotheosis: Automatically removes extra metamagic casting time.
    Can expend spell slots to conserve charges from items such as wands and staves;
    three levels of spell slots conserve one charge.
  - Bloodline spells: Identify, invisibility, dispel magic, minor globe, feeblemind,
    true seeing, teleport, power word stun, timestop.
  - Bonus feat choices: Combat Casting, Improved Counterspell, Improved Initiative, Iron
    Will, Scribe Scroll, Skill Focus, Spell Focus, Still Spell.

- 1) DRACONIC
  - Focus: Dragon heritage, elemental damage and physical defenses.
  - Class skill: Perception.
  - Heritage suboptions: Determines breath energy, energy resistance, elemental claw
    damage and the matching-spell damage bonus.
    - 1) Black: Acid.
    - 2) Blue: Electricity.
    - 3) Green: Poison in this codebase.
    - 4) Red: Fire.
    - 5) White: Cold.
    - 6) Brass: Fire.
    - 7) Bronze: Electricity.
    - 8) Copper: Acid.
    - 9) Silver: Cold.
    - 10) Gold: Fire.
  - Level 1 - Draconic Arcana: Matching-energy spells gain +1 damage per die.
  - Level 1 - Draconic Claws: Two attacks dealing 1d4 + Strength bonus each.
    - Uses: 3 + Charisma modifier per five minutes.
    - Level 7: Claws count as magical for damage reduction.
    - Level 9: Base damage increases to 1d6.
    - Level 11: Each hit adds 1d6 heritage energy damage.
  - Level 3 - Draconic Resistances: Heritage energy resistance 5 and natural armor +1.
    - Level 9: Resistance 10 and natural armor +2.
    - Level 15: Natural armor +4.
  - Level 9 - Draconic Breath Weapon: 1d6 heritage energy damage per level.
  - Level 15 - Draconic Wings: Extend or retract wings for flight speed 60.
  - Level 20 - Power of Wyrms: Sleep, paralysis and heritage energy immunity; gains
    blindsense.
  - Bloodline spells: Mage armor, resist energy, fly, wizard eye, telekinesis, true
    seeing, waves of exhaustion, mass domination, polymorph.
  - Bonus feat choices: Blind Fight, Great Fortitude, Improved Initiative, Power Attack,
    Quicken Spell, Skill Focus, Toughness.
  - Commands: dracclaws, dracbreath, dracwings.
  - Restriction: Dragon Disciples must choose Draconic.

- 2) FEY
  - Focus: Enchantment, concealment and wilderness magic.
  - Class skill: Survival.
  - Heritage suboptions: None.
  - Level 1 - Fey Arcana: Enchantment spells gain +2 save DC.
  - Level 1 - Laughing Touch: Target can take only a move action for one round. Swift
    action; 3 + Charisma modifier uses per five minutes.
  - Level 3 - Wilderness Stride: Reduces wilderness movement penalties.
  - Level 9 - Fleeting Glance: Greater invisibility three times per five minutes.
  - Level 15 - Fey Magic: Roll twice to overcome spell resistance and keep the better
    result.
  - Level 20 - Soul of the Fey: Poison immunity, +3 damage reduction, animals do not
    automatically attack you, and shadow walk once per five minutes.
  - Bloodline spells: Charm, hideous laughter, deep slumber, charm monster, feeblemind,
    true seeing, prismatic spray, irresistible dance, polymorph.
  - Bonus feat choices: Dodge, Improved Initiative, Lightning Reflexes, Mobility, Point
    Blank Shot, Precise Shot, Quicken Spell, Skill Focus.
  - Commands: fey, feymagic.

- 3) UNDEAD
  - Focus: Fear, grave magic and undead defenses.
  - Class skill: Intimidate.
  - Heritage suboptions: None.
  - Level 1 - Undead Arcana: Your mind-affecting spells can affect undead.
  - Level 1 - Grave Touch: Move-action touch causes shaken for half your level in
    rounds. Uses: 3 + Charisma modifier per five minutes.
  - Level 3 - Death's Gift: Cold resistance 20, nonlethal resistance 30 and DR 1/-.
    - Level 9: Cold resistance 40, nonlethal resistance 50 and DR 2/-.
  - Level 9 - Grasp of the Dead: Skeletal arms attack enemies in the room for 1d6
    slashing damage per level. Reflex halves; failed saves also prevent movement for one
    round. Requires a solid surface.
    - Save DC: 10 + half sorcerer level + Charisma modifier.
    - Uses: 1 per five minutes, 2 per five minutes at level 17, 3 per five minutes at
      level 20.
  - Level 15 - Incorporeal Form: Become incorporeal for three rounds, generally halving
    incoming non-spell damage and your outgoing physical damage against corporeal
    targets. Ghost touch and incorporeal opponents affect these rules. Uses: sorcerer
    level divided by 3 per five minutes.
  - Level 20 - One of Us: Cold and nonlethal immunity, DR 5/-, sleep and paralysis
    immunity, and +4 morale on saves against undead spellcasters. Undead NPCs do not
    automatically attack you.
  - Bloodline spells: Chill touch, false life, vampiric touch, animate dead, waves of
    fatigue, undeath to death, finger of death, horrid wilting, energy drain.
  - Bonus feat choices: Combat Casting, Diehard, Endurance, Iron Will, Spell Focus,
    Still Spell, Toughness, Skill Focus.

- 4) SHADOW
  - Focus: Stealth, darkness and shadow control.
  - Class skill: Stealth.
  - Heritage suboptions: None.
  - Level 1 - Shadow Arcana: Casting darkness, shadow shield, shadow walk or shadow jump
    grants a competence Stealth bonus equal to spell circle for 5 minutes.
  - Level 1 - Shadowstrike: Melee touch deals 1d4 + half level damage. Targets without
    darkvision or low-light vision are dazzled for ten rounds. Cannot reduce a target
    below 1 HP. Undead and constructs are immune. Uses: 3 + Charisma bonus per five
    minutes, minimum 1. Uses a swift action.
  - Level 3 - Nighteye: See in natural darkness.
  - Level 9 - Shadow Well: Draw shadows around yourself or a grouped ally, making their
    outline difficult to follow.
    - Command: bloodline shadow-well [group ally] - defaults to yourself.
    - Requirement: You and the target must be in the same dark room when activated.
    - Effect: Grants 50% concealment against attacks for half your sorcerer level in
      rounds.
    - Uses: 1 per five minutes, increasing to 2 per five minutes at level 17 and 3 per
      five minutes at level 20.
    - Action: Standard action.
    - Stacking: Uses the strongest applicable concealment effect; does not add another
      50% to existing concealment.
    - Counters: Uses the game's normal concealment bypass rules. Does not prevent area
      damage or effects that do not check concealment.
    - Existing passive benefit: Retain the ability to hide while observed in darkness.
  - Level 15 - Enveloping Darkness: Darkens the room and entangles eligible enemies for
    15 rounds; you see through the darkness. Freedom of movement prevents the
    entanglement. Once per five minutes.
  - Level 20 - Shadow Master: See through magical darkness, gain +2 illusion save DC and
    extend shadow shield duration by 20%.
  - Bonus spells: Ray of enfeeblement, infravision, darkness, greater invisibility,
    shadow shield, shadow walk, power word blind, scint pattern, weird.
  - Bonus feat choices: Acrobatic, Blind Fight, Dodge, Quick Draw, Silent Spell, Skill
    Focus, Stealthy, Weapon Finesse.
  - Commands:
    - bloodline shadowstrike <enemy>
    - bloodline shadow-well <group ally>
    - bloodline enveloping-darkness

- 5) STORMBORN
  - Focus: Electricity, thunder and storm mobility.
  - Class skill: Nature.
  - Heritage suboptions: None.
  - Level 1 - Stormborn Arcana: Electricity and sonic spells gain +1 save DC.
  - Level 1 - Thunderstaff: Your or a grouped ally's wielded melee attacks gain +1d6
    electricity damage for half your level in rounds, minimum 1.
    - From level 9: Critical hits add another 1d10 electricity
    - Uses: 3 + Charisma bonus per five minutes, minimum 1.
  - Level 3 - Stormchild: Electricity and sonic resistance 5, and protection against
    wind knockdown, including +4 defense against other forms of knockdown.
  - Level 9: See through fog and mist. Combat target's concealment is reduced by 10% for
    you alone.
  - Level 9 - Thunderbolt: Room attack deals 1d6/level, capped at 30d6, split between
    electricity and sonic. Reflex halves; failed saves also deafen for one round.
    - Save DC: 10 + half sorcerer level + Charisma bonus.
    - Uses: 1 per five minutes, 2 per five minutes at level 17, 3 per five minutes at
      level 20.
  - Level 15 - Ride the Lightning: Travel through one legal adjacent exit and release a
    thunderbolt on arrival. Requires being out of combat; once per five minutes.
    Respects doors and normal movement restrictions. Cannot pass blocker mobs you
    normally couldn't pass.
  - Level 20 - Storm Lord: Stun, deafness and wind-knockdown immunity. Automatically
    absorbs the first electricity or sonic damage and heals one-third of its remaining
    damage after defenses. On a 5 minute cooldown.
  - Bonus spells: Shocking grasp, gust of wind, lightning bolt, thunderclap, overland
    flight, chain lightning, control weather, whirlwind, storm of vengeance.
  - Bonus feat choices: Deadly Aim, Dodge, Enlarge Spell, Far Shot, Great Fortitude,
    Point Blank Shot, Skill Focus, Mobility.
  - Commands:
    - bloodline thunderstaff [group ally]
    - bloodline thunderbolt
    - bloodline ride-the-lightning <direction>

- 6) DESTINED
  - Focus: Luck, ally support, rerolls and survival.
  - Class skill: History.
  - Heritage suboptions: None.
  - Level 1 - Destined Arcana: A personal-range sorcerer ability grants a luck bonus to
    saves equal to its circle for one minute. Usable once per 5 mins.
  - Level 1 - Touch of Destiny: Yourself or a grouped ally gains an insight bonus equal
    to half your level, minimum 1, on attacks, saves and skill checks for 3 rounds.
    Uses: 3 + Charisma bonus per five minutes, minimum 1.
  - Level 3 - Fated: +1 luck to saves, and to AC while flat-footed or unaware. Increases
    at levels 7, 11, 15 and 19, reaching +5.
  - Level 9 - It Was Meant to Be: Every d20 roll has a 1% chance to reroll, keeping the
    second result. Passive; no arming required. Maximum procs per five minutes: 1,
    increasing to 2 at level 10 and 3 at level 20.
  - Level 15 - Within Reach: Once per five minutes, lethal damage triggers a DC 20 Will
    save. Success leaves you at 1 HP; failure still spends the use.
  - Level 20 - Destiny Realized: Criticals against you require a natural 20
    confirmation. Your spell critical threats automatically confirm. 5% chance on a
    failed spell resistance check to reroll the check.
  - Bonus spells: True strike, blur, protection from energy, free movement, greater
    dispelling, greater heroism, spell turning, protection from spells, timestop.
  - Bonus feat choices: Combat Casting, Diehard, Endurance, Leadership, Lightning
    Reflexes, Maximize Spell, Skill Focus, Weapon Focus.
  - Commands:
    - bloodline touch-of-destiny [group ally]
    - Reroll and penetration now trigger passively; legacy commands explain this.

- 7) ABYSSAL
  - Focus: Demonic claws, Strength and enhanced summons.
  - Class skill: Arcana.
  - Heritage suboptions: None.
  - Level 1 - Abyssal Arcana: Summoned creatures gain 5/good DR, bypassed by
    good-aligned attacks. Normal non-stacking DR rules apply.
  - Level 1 - Abyssal Claws: Swift action with both hands free makes two claw attacks,
    each dealing 1d4 + Strength bonus.
    - Level 5: Claws count as magical for damage reduction.
    - Level 7: Base damage increases to 1d6.
    - Level 11: Each hit adds 1d6 fire damage.
    - Smaller bodies use smaller damage dice.
    - Uses: 3 + Charisma bonus per five minutes, minimum 1.
  - Level 3 - Demon Resistances: Electricity resistance 5 and +2 poison saves.
    - Level 9: Resistance 10 and poison save bonus +4.
  - Level 9 - Strength of the Abyss: +2 inherent Strength, improving to +4 at level 13
    and +6 at level 17. Normal stat caps and inherent bonus stacking rules apply.
  - Level 15 - Added Summonings: Summon creature spells calling evil aligned creatures
    gain +2 to AC, attack and damage.
  - Level 20 - Demonic Might: Electricity and poison immunity; acid, cold and fire
    resistance 10; universal speech and comprehension.
  - Bonus spells: Cause fear, strength, rage, stoneskin, dismissal, transformation,
    teleport, protection from spells, summon creature IX.
  - Bonus feat choices: Augment Summoning, Cleave, Empower Spell, Great Fortitude,
    Improved Bull Rush, Improved Sunder, Power Attack, Skill Focus.
  - Command: bloodline abyssal-claws <enemy>.
  - Alignment: No requirement.

- 8) ELEMENTAL
  - Focus: Energy specialization, conversion and elemental movement.
  - Class skill: Arcana.
  - Heritage suboptions: Permanent choice determining energy attacks, conversion,
    resistance, immunity and level-15 movement.
    - 1) Air:
      - Energy: Electricity.
      - Level 15: Permanent flight at speed 60.
    - 2) Earth:
      - Energy: Acid.
      - Level 15: Land movement cost reduced by 10 points; ignores difficult terrain.
        Normal minimum movement costs still apply. Uses legal exits and respects doors.
        Does not burrow through water, lava, open-air sectors or walls.
    - 3) Fire:
      - Energy: Fire.
      - Level 15: +30 ground speed.
    - 4) Water:
      - Energy: Cold.
      - Level 15: Natural swimming at speed 60. Does not grant water breathing or bypass
        boat-only sectors.
  - Level 1 - Elemental Arcana: Toggle conversion of your sorcerer spells' fire, cold,
    acid, electricity or sonic damage into your heritage energy. Burning hands and
    scorching ray always use heritage energy.
  - Level 1 - Elemental Ray: Ranged touch deals 1d6 + half level heritage energy damage.
    Uses: 3 + Charisma bonus per five minutes, minimum 1. Uses a swift action.
  - Level 3 - Elemental Resistance: Heritage energy resistance 10, increasing to 20 at
    level 9.
  - Level 9 - Elemental Blast: Room attack deals 1d6/level, capped at 30d6. Reflex
    halves. Failed saves cause 50% vulnerability to heritage energy for two rounds,
    applied after the blast.
    - Save DC: 10 + half sorcerer level + Charisma bonus.
    - Uses: 1 per five minutes, 2 per five minutes at level 17, 3 per five minutes at
      level 20.
  - Level 15 - Elemental Movement: Grants the benefit listed under heritage.
  - Level 20 - Elemental Body: Immunity to heritage energy, critical hits and sneak
    attacks, using native immunity rules.
  - Bonus spells: Burning hands, scorching ray, protection from energy, stoneskin, fire
    shield, transformation, spell mantle, summon creature VIII, elemental swarm.
  - Bonus feat choices: Dodge, Empower Spell, Great Fortitude, Improved Initiative,
    Lightning Reflexes, Power Attack, Skill Focus, Weapon Finesse.
  - Commands:
    - bloodline energy-conversion [on/off]
    - bloodline elemental-ray <enemy>
    - bloodline elemental-blast

- 9) CELESTIAL
  - Focus: Protective summons, healing, wings and conviction.
  - Class skill: Medicine.
  - Heritage suboptions: None.
  - Level 1 - Celestial Arcana: Summoned creatures gain DR 5/evil, bypassed by
    evil-aligned attacks. Normal non-stacking DR rules apply.
  - Level 1 - Heavenly Fire: Ranged touch deals 1d12 + half level holy damage to an evil
    enemy, using native holy defenses. Instead heals a good grouped ally, or yourself if
    good. Neutral targets are unaffected.
    - Each recipient can receive healing once per five minutes across all casters. Owned
      companions share their player master's receipt limit.
    - Uses: 3 + Charisma bonus per five minutes, minimum 1. Uses a swift action.
  - Level 3 - Celestial Resistances: Acid and cold resistance 5, increasing to 10 at
    level 9.
  - Level 9 - Wings of Heaven: Flight speed 60 for ten rounds per activation. Uses per
    five minutes equal your sorcerer level. Wings can be retracted.
  - Level 15 - Conviction: Arm a reroll of your next attack, skill check or saving throw
    within ten rounds. Swift action. You can specify whether it applies to an attack
    roll, saving throw or skill check. Other roll categories do not consume it. Defaults
    to any eligible category. Keep the second result, even if worse. Usable once per 30
    seconds.
  - Level 20 - Ascension: Acid and cold immunity, fire and electricity resistance 10, +4
    poison saves, universal speech and comprehension, and unlimited Wings of Heaven.
  - Bonus spells: Bless, resist energy, circle against evil, remove curse, flame strike,
    greater dispelling, banish, sunburst, gate.
  - Bonus feat choices: Dodge, Extend Spell, Iron Will, Mobility, Mounted Combat,
    Ride-By Attack, Skill Focus, Weapon Finesse.
  - Commands:
    - bloodline heavenly-fire <target>
    - bloodline wings [off]
    - bloodline conviction [attack/save/skill/any]
  - Alignment/deity: No requirement. Celestial influence is inherited innate magic.

- Additional implementation notes
  - The new bloodlines adapt Pathfinder powers to the existing MUD's rooms, combat,
    movement and spell catalogue; some bonus spells are substitutions.
  - Bloodline powers respect normal legal-target, group, peaceful-room, action and
    movement rules as applicable.
  - Type bloodline to list unlocked powers and uses per five minutes for the six new
    bloodlines. Use feat info <power name> for descriptions.
  - New power cooldowns and Heavenly Fire receipt limits survive logout.
  - Abyssal and Celestial summon wards are reapplied to restored living conjured
    companions with fixed DR 5 and the owner's applicable summon bonuses. Purchased
    pets, undead and constructs are excluded from this restoration bonus.
