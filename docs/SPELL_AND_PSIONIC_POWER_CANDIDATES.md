# Proposed New Spells and Psionic Powers

Review shortlist: **20 spells and 10 psionic powers**. The suggested first batch (eight spells and five powers) is now implemented; see [class access, commands and deployment notes](SPELL_POWER_FIRST_BATCH_SETUP.txt). The second batch adds five spells and three powers; see [second-batch access and implementation notes](SPELL_POWER_SECOND_BATCH_SETUP.txt). The [final batch](SPELL_POWER_FINAL_BATCH_SETUP.txt) implements the remaining seven spells and two powers. All 30 candidates are now implemented with the documented MUD adaptations.

Selections were compared against the current registrations in `src/spell_parser.c`, `src/psionics.c`, and the identifiers in `src/spells.h`. At the time of the original shortlist, none of these names was registered there. Some deliberately share existing effects while offering a different combination, target, or spell/power progression.

Descriptions below summarize the linked tabletop sources in original wording. **MUD fit** records the original proposal; the batch setup guides describe the implemented adaptations and omissions. Damage, costs, durations, class access, and stacking remain subject to balancing. **Source access** gives tabletop spell/power levels. **Proposed MUD access** gives levels in the named class, not total multiclass character level. These unlocks are implemented for all three batches. They do not grant automatic learning.

## How Class Access Was Chosen

The source-access columns are taken from each entry's linked rules page. They include source classes that the MUD does not currently offer, and sometimes domain or mystery access. These do not imply new MUD classes, domains, or mysteries are being implemented.

Proposed MUD unlocks follow the existing class progression in `src/class.c`:

| MUD class | Spell/power circles used here → class level |
|---|---|
| Wizard, Cleric, Druid | 1 → 1; 2 → 3; 3 → 5; 4 → 7; 5 → 9 |
| Sorcerer | 1 → 1; 2 → 4; 3 → 6; 4 → 8; 5 → 10 |
| Bard, Alchemist, Inquisitor | 1 → 1; 2 → 4; 3 → 7; 4 → 10; 5 → 13 |
| Paladin, Ranger, Blackguard | 1 → 6; 2 → 10; 3 → 12; 4 → 15 |
| Psionicist | 1 → 1; 2 → 3; 3 → 5; 4 → 7; 5 → 9 |

- **Blackguard** is the proposed MUD recipient for source **Antipaladin** spells. This is an explicit adaptation.
- **Psionicist** is the proposed recipient of all ten powers. Egoist, Kineticist, Seer, Shaper, and Telepath remain source specialist labels; this shortlist does not introduce specialist subclasses or grant these powers to arcane/divine casters.
- Source classes without a corresponding MUD base class are left out of the proposed-access column. In particular, source **Psychic** spell access is not converted into Psionicist access.
- Prestige classes that advance another class's spellcasting should use that existing progression. They are not assigned separate direct unlocks in this list.
- Alchemist entries are proposed extracts through its existing casting mechanics. Source domain, subdomain, and mystery listings are references, not additional proposed grants.
- A listed class level is the earliest proposed access; ordinary spell learning, preparation, known-power selection, ability requirements, and other existing restrictions still apply.

## Suggested First Batch

For the closest match to existing mechanics, begin discussion with **Ear-Piercing Scream, Frigid Touch, Frost Fall, Burst of Radiance, Holy Smite, Unholy Blight, Order's Wrath, and Chaos Hammer**; and **Energy Missile, Energy Cone, Destiny Dissonance, Reconstruction, and Hail of Crystals**.

The other candidates add more weapon interactions, ongoing combat hooks, or tactical choices. None requires hunger or thirst, free-form world creation, character duplication, or time reversal.
