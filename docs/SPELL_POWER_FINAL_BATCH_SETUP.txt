FINAL SPELL AND PSIONIC POWER BATCH

Completes the original shortlist with seven spells and two powers. All 20 spells
and 10 powers are now implemented. This guide records the native MUD adaptations;
source tabletop descriptions remain linked in SPELL_AND_PSIONIC_POWER_CANDIDATES.md.

CLASS ACCESS

Defending Bone: Cleric 3, Inquisitor 4, Wizard 3, Sorcerer 4.
Righteous Might: Cleric 9, Inquisitor 13.
Thorn Body: Alchemist 7, Druid 7.
Vine Strike: Alchemist 4, Blackguard 10, Druid 3, Ranger 10, Wizard 3, Sorcerer 4.
Dazzling Blade: Bard 1, Wizard 1, Sorcerer 1.
Debilitating Portent: Cleric 7.
Blessing of Fervor: Cleric 7.
Crystalline Bonds: Psionicist 7; 7 PSP; augmentation in steps of 2 PSP.
Battlesense: Psionicist 5; 5 PSP; augmentation in steps of 2 PSP, maximum 8 extra.

Levels mean levels in that class, not total multiclass level. Normal learning,
preparation, known-power selection, ability requirements and resource costs apply.

Use spellchoice alone for syntax. Preferences affect subsequent casts, never an
existing effect. They reset on login to fervor defense, battlesense attack, and
bonds damage. Augmentation still obeys existing manifester-level/PSP limits.

MECHANICAL NOTES AND ADAPTATIONS

Defending Bone is self only. It grants DR 5/bludgeoning with an absorption pool of
5 per caster level, capped at 50, lasting 1200 rounds per level (capped at 30000).
Unarmed bludgeoning and appropriate bludgeoning weapons bypass it. Pool absorption
cannot exceed the pool remaining. The bone cannot be cast while another native
flat or bypassable DR source is present, and is suppressed if one appears later.
It uses normal native DR bypass/penetration and is removed when the pool expires.

Righteous Might is self only, for one round per caster level. It gives one size
category, +4 Strength and Constitution, -2 Dexterity, +2 natural armor, and DR 5,
which becomes DR 10 at caster level 15. Evil casters and negative channelers gain
DR/good; other casters gain DR/evil. It excludes Enlarge Person, Shrink Person and
Augment Physicality in both directions. Native shape and equipment calculations
apply: carried/worn equipment grows, then reverts when dropped or transferred.
Saved base equipment sizes and movement speed do not change.

Thorn Body lasts one round per caster level. Close melee hits provoke 1d6 + caster
level piercing retaliation (level bonus capped at 15). Reach weapons and ranged
attacks are exempt. Successfully grappling the caster, or hitting while in that
grapple, instead deals 2d6 + the capped level bonus. Natural and unarmed hits gain
1d6 damage. Retaliation does not recursively trigger another thorn retaliation.

Vine Strike lasts 20 rounds per caster level. The first successful natural/unarmed
hit each round gains 1d6 and attempts entanglement. The once-per-round choice is
the native substitute for selecting one particular natural weapon. Reflex avoids
entanglement and grants one round of protection against further vine entanglement;
spell resistance applies to the restraint. Failed saves entangle for the buff's
remaining duration. STRUGGLE removes these vines for a standard action, and later
hits can entangle again. Incorporeal targets cannot be entangled. Free Movement
and Slip the Bonds prevent the restraint.

Dazzling Blade enchants the caster's currently wielded metal weapon for 20 rounds
per level. Feints and disarm offense/defense gain +1, plus +1 per three caster levels,
up to +5 at level 12. The same weapon must remain wielded for its bonus to work.
The effect belongs to the caster; transferring the weapon does not transfer it.
SPELLCHOICE BLADE enemy costs a swift action and consumes the enchantment to blind
for one round, Will changing blindness to dazzling. Spell resistance and mental/
sight immunity apply. This discharge obeys normal room/AoE/group/PK protections.
The enchantment does not make the weapon silver.

Debilitating Portent lasts one round per caster level. It allows no initial save,
but spell resistance and mind-affecting immunity apply. Each outgoing normal damage
packet allows Will against 10 + half caster level + caster Wisdom bonus; failure
halves that packet. The native model rolls separately for different targets or
packets, rather than once for an entire multi-target spell. Self damage, Thorn Body
retaliation and Crystalline Bonds periodic damage do not receive this reduction.
SPELLCHOICE PORTENT enemy arms the caster's existing omen for a swift action. It
then consumes the omen on that enemy's next confirmed physical or touch critical,
preventing the critical multiplier and halving the hit. This explicit arming rule
replaces the tabletop immediate-action reaction prompt.

Blessing of Fervor affects the caster and grouped allies present in the room for
one round per caster level. Group membership is treated as consent. The caster
chooses one shared mode before casting, fixed for that cast:
  defense: +2 attack, +2 dodge AC, +2 Reflex.
  speed: +30 feet of movement speed.
  attack: one extra full-attack strike, sharing the existing Haste/speed-weapon cap.
  rise: standing uses a swift action and avoids standing-provoked opportunity attacks.
Haste suppresses Fervor's bonuses while active; neither effect is destroyed. Normal
dodge defenses apply. There is no per-round prompt or spell-modification mode.

Crystalline Bonds uses initial Reflex negates and no power resistance. Free
Movement and Slip the Bonds block it. It affects
Medium or smaller corporeal targets by default. Initial damage is 3d6 piercing;
subsequent positive-duration rounds deal 1d6 for one round per manifester level.
Every two extra PSP add +1 to the save and escape DC. In damage mode each step adds
1d6 recurring damage; in size mode each step instead permits one larger size category.
The creature is entangled, gagged, and unable to make normal attacks or ordinary
spells; it is not paralyzed. Mental powers remain possible with Concentration DC
20 + half the last continuous damage actually dealt. STRUGGLE costs a standard
action and rolls d20 + the better of Strength modifier and Escape Artist against
DC 20 + augmentation steps. Bonds are a dispellable affect, not a separate object
that allies can attack. Recurring damage uses native AoE/group/PK protections and
is suppressed in peaceful/no-magic rooms, when the caster is absent, and after a
process restart. Restraint can still be escaped or dispelled if damage is suppressed.

Battlesense affects the caster and grouped allies present for one round per level.
They retain the effect when leaving the room. Mind-affecting immunity prevents it.
Choose attack (+1), armor (+1 AC), damage (+2 weapon damage), or speed (+5 feet).
Each two extra PSP repeats that same benefit, up to five copies: +5 attack/AC,
+10 weapon damage or +25 feet. This adaptation uses willing group membership instead
of a collective. It provides one scalable chosen benefit, without mixed choices
or an immediate Aid Another action.

BUILD, DEPLOYMENT AND SQL

  cmake --build /tmp/luminari-religion-build -j4
  python3 tests/run_spell_batch.py
  python3 tests/run_spell_batch_final.py
  python3 tests/run_spellhelp.py --sanitize

New native implementation: src/spell_batch_final.c and .h, integrated into magic,
combat, movement, casting, groups, grapple escape, size handling and command dispatch.
Both CMakeLists.txt and Makefile.am include the new module.

Rebuild and restart the server to load registrations and native SPELLHELP entries.
No SQL migrations or help-table imports are required. Existing saved IDs remain
unchanged. Public spells use 555-561; internal affects use 562-564. Public powers
use 1615-1616; 1617 is an internal recurring-damage identifier.

Thorn Body, Vine Strike and Crystalline Bonds use direct native damage paths;
global spell-damage settings do not multiply their listed dice.

The native rules and help harnesses run with address/undefined-behavior sanitizers.
Live gameplay verification requires restarting the dev server on port 4301.
