FIRST SPELL AND PSIONIC POWER BATCH

Implements the suggested first batch from SPELL_AND_PSIONIC_POWER_CANDIDATES.md:
eight spells and five powers. Levels below mean levels in that class, not total
multiclass character level. Normal learning, preparation, known-power selection,
ability requirements, and resource costs still apply.

SPELLS AND CLASS ACCESS

Ear-Piercing Scream: Wizard 1, Sorcerer 1, Bard 1, Inquisitor 1.
Frigid Touch: Wizard 3, Sorcerer 4, Druid 3.
Frost Fall: Wizard 3, Sorcerer 4, Druid 3.
Burst of Radiance: Wizard 3, Sorcerer 4, Cleric 3, Druid 3.
Holy Smite: Cleric 7, Inquisitor 10.
Unholy Blight: Cleric 7, Inquisitor 10.
Order's Wrath: Cleric 7, Inquisitor 10.
Chaos Hammer: Cleric 7, Inquisitor 10.

POWERS AND CLASS ACCESS

Destiny Dissonance: Psionicist 1; base cost 1 PSP; no augmentation.
Energy Missile: Psionicist 3; base cost 3 PSP; augment in steps of 1 PSP.
Energy Cone: Psionicist 5; base cost 5 PSP; augment in steps of 1 PSP.
Reconstruction: Psionicist 3; base cost 3 PSP; augment in steps of 2 PSP.
Hail of Crystals: Psionicist 9; base cost 9 PSP; augment in steps of 1 PSP.

The registered casting name is "orders wrath", without an apostrophe, because
CAST uses single quotes to delimit spell names. SPELLHELP also accepts the
source spelling "order's wrath". All other names match the examples above.

MECHANICAL NOTES

* All room attacks use the existing group, contained-AoE, and PK protections.
  Energy Missile strikes at most five eligible targets, once each. Energy Cone
  uses the same room-wide area model as existing cone spells.
* Alignment bursts protect their matching alignment. Opposed targets take full
  damage; targets neutral on the relevant axis take half and no condition.
  A successful save halves that damage again and prevents the condition.
* Burst of Radiance can affect non-evil enemies' sight, but damages only evil
  creatures. Its Reflex save changes blindness to dazzling, not damage.
* Frigid Touch rolls a melee touch. A critical touch doubles its damage dice and
  extends staggering to ten rounds. Spellstrike does not roll another touch.
* Frost Fall initially deals 2d6 cold and can stagger for one round. One patch
  per room remains for max(1, caster level / 2) rounds; later pulses deal 1d6 cold
  without stagger. Recasting replaces the patch. Pulses stop when the caster
  leaves or is extracted, or the room becomes peaceful or no-magic. Periodic
  pulses do not retain the original cast's metamagic. Room LOOK shows the frost.
* Reconstruction repairs only surviving constructs without the listed golem
  magic-immunity feats. It uses the existing construct-repair path, avoiding the
  generic golem half-healing penalty. It cannot repair objects or resurrect.
* Hail of Crystals has a ranged-touch direct impact followed by an explosion,
  even when the impact misses or kills its target. The explosion uses normal
  area protections and allows a Reflex save. Neither stage allows PR.
* New ordinary powers use explicit epic metadata rather than an old numeric-ID
  cutoff, preserving support from ordinary-power epic feats.
* Class circle reporting now uses exclusive level bands, so first-circle Bard
  and Inquisitor spells are not mislabeled as fifth-circle in SPELLHELP.

DEPLOYMENT

Rebuild the server and restart it to load registrations and class assignments.
SPELLHELP text is native data in src/spellhelp.c and follows that same build.
No SQL migration or help-table import is required. Existing saved spell IDs have
not been renumbered. Later additions are documented in SPELL_POWER_SECOND_BATCH_SETUP.txt
and SPELL_POWER_FINAL_BATCH_SETUP.txt; the original shortlist is now complete.

FOCUSED VALIDATION

  python3 tests/run_spell_batch.py
  python3 tests/run_spellhelp.py --sanitize

These cover the new native rule blocks, alignment and condition behavior, touch
misses/criticals, augmentation, target caps, construct eligibility, hail stages,
frost caster lifetime/state restoration, ordinary-power epic support, exact class
assignments, production registrations, circle reporting and SPELLHELP rendering.
