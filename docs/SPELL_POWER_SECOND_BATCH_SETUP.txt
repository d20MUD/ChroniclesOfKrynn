SECOND SPELL AND PSIONIC POWER BATCH

Adds five spells and three psionic powers from the candidate guide.
Levels mean levels in the named class, not total character level. Normal learning,
preparation, known-power selection, ability requirements and resource costs apply.

SPELLS AND CLASS ACCESS

Burning Disarm: Cleric 1, Druid 1, Wizard 1, Sorcerer 1.
Boneshaker: Blackguard 6, Cleric 3, Inquisitor 4, Wizard 3, Sorcerer 4.
Boneshatter: Cleric 9, Wizard 7, Sorcerer 8.
Stricken Heart: Inquisitor 4, Wizard 3, Sorcerer 4.
Burst of Speed: Alchemist 7, Blackguard 12, Inquisitor 7, Paladin 12, Ranger 12.

POWERS AND CLASS ACCESS

Natural Healing: Psionicist 1; base cost 1 PSP; augment in steps of 1 PSP.
Corrosive Aura: Psionicist 1; base cost 1 PSP; augment in steps of 1 PSP.
Expose Weakness: Psionicist 5; base cost 5 PSP; optional augmentation of 6 PSP.
Existing manifester-level and power-point limits still cap augmentation.

COMMAND EXAMPLES

  spellhelp burning disarm
  spellhelp boneshaker
  spellhelp boneshatter
  spellhelp stricken heart
  spellhelp burst of speed
  spellhelp natural healing
  spellhelp corrosive aura
  spellhelp expose weakness

  cast 'burning disarm' target
  cast 'boneshaker' target
  cast 'boneshatter' target
  cast 'stricken heart' target
  cast 'burst of speed'

  manifest 0 'natural healing'
  manifest 2 'natural healing'
  manifest 0 'corrosive aura'
  manifest 2 'corrosive aura'
  manifest 0 'expose weakness' target
  manifest 6 'expose weakness' target

MECHANICAL NOTES

Burning Disarm heats the first held/wielded conductive metal item. It deals
1d4 fire per caster level, capped at 5d4. A successful Reflex save instead unequips
the item intact into its holder's inventory and avoids all damage. The item stays
with its owner, following normal disarm custody, rather than becoming room loot. ITEM_NODROP items stay
equipped and permit no save. Underwater damage is halved. Spell resistance applies.

Boneshaker deals (3 + caster level / 2)d6 bludgeoning damage, Fortitude half.
It requires a corporeal living skeleton, excluding oozes, plants, elementals,
constructs and vermin. This batch implements the guide's damage adaptation;
it does not reposition creatures or force undead actions.

Boneshatter deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage per caster level, capped at 15d6.
A successful Fortitude save halves damage and fatigues the target; failure
exhausts it. The condition lasts caster level * 10 rounds. Native fatigue affects
attack, armor and damage. Exhaustion additionally gives -4 Strength, -4 Dexterity
and half movement speed. Slow and exhaustion do not compound movement halving.
A weaker recast cannot overwrite exhaustion. Requires a corporeal creature with
bones or carapace, including undead and vermin; excludes oozes, plants, elementals
and constructs. Does not damage world objects. Spell resistance applies.

Stricken Heart requires a melee touch against a living target and deals 2d6
negative damage with no save. It staggers for one round. A critical touch deals
4d6 and staggers for ten rounds. Death-magic immunity blocks the spell;
critical/precision immunity prevents staggering. Spell resistance applies.
Spellstrike uses the successful weapon hit rather than rolling another touch.

Burst of Speed lasts one round. It adds 20 feet of movement speed, reduced to
10 feet in medium/heavy armor. Movement-provoked attacks of opportunity are
suppressed while active. Casting, combat maneuvers and other actions can still
provoke. It grants no extra attacks. Mounted movement uses the mount's speed.

Natural Healing restores 3 hit points to the living manifester, plus 3 per extra
power point. Normal healing modifiers and maximum hit points apply. It cannot
heal another creature, constructs or undead.

Corrosive Aura lasts one round per manifester level. Each positive-duration
round deals 1d6 acid to eligible enemies sharing the manifester's current room;
each extra power point adds 1d6. Every two extra points add +1 save DC. Reflex
halves each pulse; power resistance applies. Uses the existing room-based AoE,
group and PK protections. Peaceful/no-magic rooms suppress pulses. The aura
moves with its caster and stores its own augmentation independently of later
manifestations. It does not immediately pulse on casting, and periodic pulses
do not retain metamagic.

Expose Weakness lasts one round per manifester level, Fortitude negates and
power resistance applies. All attackers treat the target as flanked, subject
to existing flanking defenses. They gain +4 to critical confirmation. Existing
probabilistic fortification must succeed twice to block, reducing a 25% block
to 6.25%; unconditional critical/precision immunity remains intact. Spending
6 extra power points makes the target flat-footed instead of granting automatic
flanking. Native uncanny-dodge defenses still apply. The augmented flat-footed
state persists for the duration even after ordinary per-round flags are cleared.
A weaker recast cannot remove the augmented effect.

DEPLOYMENT

Rebuild and restart the server. SPELLHELP data is compiled into src/spellhelp.c;
no SQL migrations or help-table imports are required. Existing saved spell and
power IDs are unchanged. New spells use IDs 550-554; powers use 1611-1613.
ID 1614 is an internal aura damage handler and cannot be learned or manifested.

VALIDATION

  python3 tests/run_spell_batch.py
  python3 tests/run_spellhelp.py --sanitize
  cmake --build /tmp/luminari-religion-build -j4

The native-rule harness covers both implemented batches, with address/undefined
behavior sanitizers. The help harness covers native registrations, class levels,
public/internal filtering, detailed rendering and audit coverage. Live gameplay
verification requires a restart of the dev server.
