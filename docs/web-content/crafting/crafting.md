# Crafting Guide

Create equipment with your own descriptions, materials and magical bonuses. You can also refine resources, resize existing equipment, complete supply orders and, with the right abilities, build golems.

## Getting started

Type `craft tutorial` in game, or follow the [Crafting Tutorial](https://krynn.d20mud.com/wiki/crafting-tutorial).

```
craft score
craft tools
craft equipment
materials
```

These commands show your skills, equipped tools and stored resources. Begin with a plain item before attempting magical bonuses.

The usual crafting process is:

1. Equip the appropriate harvesting tool, find a resource area and use `survey`.
2. Use `harvest` where resources are available.
3. Refine ingredients when your chosen recipe requires it.
4. Choose your item type and variant, write its descriptions and assign materials.
5. Review with `craft show`, resolve the requirements shown by `craft check`, then use `craft start`.

## Growing as a crafter

Use `craft score` to track progress and `talents` to explore crafting talents. You can permanently specialize in up to two crafting or harvesting skills with `craft specialize <skill name>`. Each specialization gives +5 to its checks and doubles its experience gains. Choose carefully: specializations cannot be changed.

Golem construction is a separate path with construct-feat requirements. See the golem section of the tutorial and type `craft golem` for its commands.
